# T-066 "Chaos" redesign — executive click-through QA

Agents can't type passwords (RULES) so the **logged-in** screens are verified by
code + review only. This is your hand-check list. Tick each as you go; note the
theme/size where anything looks off.

Test matrix for every item below: **dark + light + a team theme**, **desktop
(≥1024px) + phone (<768px)**. Theme switch lives under the "More" sheet on phone
and in the header controls on desktop.

Demo: https://choas-league.vercel.app/l/3ca78f4e-339f-463c-9e43-6acbfae610fd
(Local: http://localhost:3000 — but logged-in screens need your account.)

## Phase 1 — foundation (uncommitted, in review)

Public screens (login, signup) — **already verified by me**, dark+light,
desktop+mobile: wordmark renders, Inter/Oswald load, accent consistent, no
h-scroll on phone. The items below are the ones I can't reach:

- [ ] **Header brand** — every league page shows the Chaos bolt mark on phone
      and the full "CHAOS LEAGUE" wordmark on desktop, left of the league
      switcher. Clicking it returns to league home.
- [ ] **Desktop tab bar** — at ≥768px a persistent horizontal row of tabs
      (icon + label) sits under the header. **No "Menu"/hamburger button.**
      The section you're on is underlined in the accent color.
- [ ] **All sections reachable in one click** from that bar: Matchups,
      Standings, Players, My Team, Trades, Log/Transactions, Chat, Draft, and
      Admin (commissioner only). None renders with a blank/missing icon.
- [ ] **Tab bar scrolls sideways** (does not wrap to a second row) on a
      narrow laptop (~1024px) if the tabs don't all fit.
- [ ] **Phone nav unchanged** — bottom bar still 4 tabs + More; no desktop
      tab bar appears below 768px.
- [ ] **Fonts** — headings and all scores are the condensed face (Oswald);
      body/labels are Inter. Nothing still looks like the old default.
- [ ] **Home matchup card** — the two scores are large and dominant; a
      finished game reads "Final" (not "FINAL").
- [ ] **Home empty states** (a brand-new/empty week) read: "No matchup on the
      board this week", "No standings yet — nobody has played a down", and
      "Quiet so far. Adds, drops and trades land here."
- [ ] **Team theme** — on a page where you own a team, surfaces take your
      franchise wash and the mark/wordmark/active tab recolor to your team
      accent and stay readable.
- [ ] No console errors; active-tab highlight follows you as you move around.

## Phase 2 — reskins

### Matchup detail (committed) — open a matchup from Home or the Matchups tab

- [ ] Each starter row shows the player's **points as the biggest element**,
      with the two sides' numbers facing off across the slot label (QB, RB…).
      Name + position/opponent trail to the outside.
- [ ] In a **final** matchup the losing side's player numbers and the losing
      team's total are **dimmed** so the winner reads at a glance. A **tie**
      dims neither side.
- [ ] **Phone (375px):** player names are readable (not cut to a few letters);
      the NFL crest is hidden on phone and returns on desktop.
- [ ] The big team scoreboard numbers up top are large (condensed face); the
      loser is dimmed; a finished game reads "Final".
- [ ] **Tap any player** → breakdown modal: every scored stat line → points,
      and the lines **add up exactly** to the shown total (nothing that
      actually scored shows as "0.0"; header/body/footer totals match).
- [ ] Breakdown header shows the right game state: **"Scheduled" before
      kickoff** (not "In progress"); "In progress" / "Final" / "BYE" otherwise.
- [ ] Bench section total and per-player bench points use the condensed face.
- [ ] Multi-week matchup: the Week/Total switcher works; per-week columns
      reconcile to the total.

### Players tab (committed) — the Players tab

- [ ] **Desktop:** dense table with a **headshot** beside each name, a colored
      **availability chip** (green "Free agent" / orange "Waivers" / team
      crest+name for rostered), and Last/Avg/Total in the condensed face.
- [ ] **Desktop:** Add/Bid opens a **centered modal** (not a bottom sheet, and
      it doesn't reflow the row) — reachable without horizontal scroll;
      add/claim/bid/drop works; FAAB vs waiver-priority header copy correct.
- [ ] **Phone (375px):** player **cards** (headshot, name, pos/team/bye,
      availability chip, Avg/Total); **no sideways scroll**.
- [ ] **Phone:** **sticky search** + horizontal **position chips** pinned at
      the top; cards scroll under the search cleanly (readable contrast, no
      collision with the nav).
- [ ] **Phone:** the **"Add player" FAB** floats above the bottom nav, opens
      the bottom sheet, and adds/bids; Esc / backdrop / close button all shut
      it; the background doesn't scroll while it's open; the FAB doesn't
      collide with the Previous/Next buttons at the bottom of a long page.
- [ ] **Headshots:** the portrait sits well inside the round frame; a player
      with no portrait shows clean **initials**, not a broken image.
- [ ] Sorting (Last/Avg/Total), pagination, and pending claims still work.

Note: projected-points column is NOT here yet (T-069 — needs a data change).

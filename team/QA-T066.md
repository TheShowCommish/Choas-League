# T-066 "Chaos" redesign — executive click-through QA

Agents can't type passwords (RULES) so the **logged-in** screens are verified by
code + review only. This is your hand-check list. Tick each as you go; note the
theme/size where anything looks off.

Test matrix for every item below: **dark + light + a team theme**, **desktop
(≥1024px) + phone (<768px)**. Theme switch lives under the "More" sheet on phone
and in the header controls on desktop.

Demo: https://choas-league.vercel.app/l/3ca78f4e-339f-463c-9e43-6acbfae610fd
(Local: http://localhost:3000 — but logged-in screens need your account.)

## Phase 2 — reskins

### Matchup detail (committed) — open a matchup from Home or the Matchups tab

- [ ] Each starter row shows the player's **points as the biggest element**,
      with the two sides' numbers facing off across the slot label (QB, RB…).
      Name + position/opponent trail to the outside.
- [ ] In a **final** matchup the losing side's player numbers and the losing
      team's total are **dimmed** so the winner reads at a glance. A **tie**
      dims neither side.
- [ ] **Phone (375px):** player names are readable (not cut to a few letters);
      the NFL crest is hidden on phone and returns on desktop.
- [ ] The big team scoreboard numbers up top are large (condensed face); the
      loser is dimmed; a finished game reads "Final".
- [ ] **Tap any player** → breakdown modal: every scored stat line → points,
      and the lines **add up exactly** to the shown total (nothing that
      actually scored shows as "0.0"; header/body/footer totals match).
- [ ] Breakdown header shows the right game state: **"Scheduled" before
      kickoff** (not "In progress"); "In progress" / "Final" / "BYE" otherwise.
- [ ] Bench section total and per-player bench points use the condensed face.
- [ ] Multi-week matchup: the Week/Total switcher works; per-week columns
      reconcile to the total.

## Live matchup projection (T-071) — matchup detail during a live (non-final) week

- [ ] On a matchup with games still to play, a "PROJECTED" strip sits under the
      scoreboard: each side's projected final, smaller than the live score and
      clearly labeled so it isn't mistaken for the live number.
- [ ] A verdict chip reads "TEAM by N.N" (or "Dead even"), and a two-color bar
      shows each side's share of projected points.
- [ ] Each side shows "N to play · N.N to come" (or "all played").
- [ ] Once every game is final the strip disappears and the projected finals
      equal the live scoreboard (reconciles).
- [ ] Phone (375px): the subline wraps without pushing the numbers into overflow;
      the two projected finals don't collide on a high-scoring game.
- [ ] The split-bar colors read clearly even for two similar franchise colors.

Note: an in-progress player uses his full pre-game projection (not live + rest),
so projected can read slightly under the live total mid-game — by design (v1).

## Roster-health alerts (T-075) — My Team + Home, as a logged-in manager

- [ ] **My Team** (own team, current/unlocked week): an empty starting slot, a
      starter on bye, or an OUT/IR starter shows a "Lineup needs attention"
      banner listing each issue (slot · player · reason). OUT/injury tag is red;
      empty/bye are accent (not alarm-red).
- [ ] **Home:** a one-line "Lineup needs attention — N issues · Review" bar
      linking to My Team (only for your own team).
- [ ] **No false alarm:** a pre-draft / freshly-claimed team (empty roster)
      shows NO banner on either page.
- [ ] Questionable / Doubtful starters do NOT trigger it (only OUT/IR/PUP/etc.).
- [ ] A past week, or once the week's games have kicked off (all locked), shows
      no banner.
- [ ] Phone (375px): long player names truncate; no sideways scroll.
- [ ] Known wrinkle: on the current week before you've saved, the My Team banner
      may list slots "Empty" while the editor below shows them auto-filled — the
      banner reflects the SAVED (scoring) lineup; saving clears it.
